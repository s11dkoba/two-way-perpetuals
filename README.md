## About

### Trading can feel scary

Perpetual trading is hard for beginners. Profit, loss, and liquidation risk are difficult to picture.

- Long and Short ideas are new to many beginners
- Leverage can make losses grow fast
- It is hard to see how close you are to liquidation
- Two-Way Perpetuals lets you practice in demo mode

### How it works

A mobile-first app with three bottom tabs: Trade, Positions, and Swap. Jupiter, Changelly, and the wallet are mocks.

- Trade BTC/USD with USDC margin and up to 3x leverage
- Open an order in a bottom sheet: margin, leverage, Long or Short
- Positions show live PnL and a safety bar for liquidation distance
- Swap BTC, ETH, or SOL to USDC with a simple card showing rate and fee

### Why it matters, what's next

You can learn how leverage and liquidation work with no real money at risk. The price simulator makes it easy.

- No real trades or funds are used in demo mode
- Price simulator buttons move the price by 3% or 10%
- Japanese and English, dark neon design, PC and phone
- Next: confirm the name and the demo starting settings
